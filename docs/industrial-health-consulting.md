﻿# Industrial Health Consulting (사이트 기반 문서)

**Summary:** Industrial health consulting services — measurements, risk assessment, ergonomics, and legal compliance support.

## Overview
This document mirrors the website content for `산업보건컨설팅` (Industrial Health Consulting) provided by the Korean Environmental & Safety Research Institute. It includes service descriptions, processes, strengths, costs, downloads, FAQ, and contact information.

### Key Points
- Support for compliance with the Serious Accidents Punishment Act (effective 2022-01-27)
- Work environment measurements (designated by Ministry of Employment and Labor)
- Risk assessment, chemical management, musculoskeletal hazard surveys, occupational medicine support

## Services

### Work Environment Measurements
- Regular statutory measurements (e.g., general hazards every 6 months)
- Measure noise, dust, VOCs, metals, etc.
- Reports follow KOSHA-A-1-2021 and other standards

### Risk Assessment
- Site diagnosis, hazard identification, risk estimation, mitigation plan

### Chemical Management
- SDS organization, exposure assessment, CRA models (ECETOC TRA, Stoffenmanager, etc.)

### Musculoskeletal Hazard Survey
- 3-year periodic survey for workplaces with 5+ employees

## Process
1. Pre-survey and scoping
2. Field survey and sampling
3. Analysis and evaluation
4. Reporting and follow-up support
